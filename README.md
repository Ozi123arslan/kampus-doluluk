# Campus Occupancy

A Flutter app that shows how crowded the cafés, dining hall and gym on YTU's Davutpaşa Campus are right now. Students who are at a venue report "empty / moderate / full" with one tap; reports are weighted by how recent they are, combined, and shown to everyone live.
